Connect your camera

Looking for the camera on your Wi-Fi network…

  1. On the camera, press Playback, then choose Wireless Communication.
  2. On this computer, join the camera's Wi-Fi network (named FUJIFILM-…).
  3. Ferry connects automatically. Press OK on the camera if it asks.
