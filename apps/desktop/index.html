Fuji Import

Connect your camera

Looking for the camera on your Wi-Fi network…

  1. On the camera, press Playback, then choose Wireless Communication.
  2. On this computer, join the camera's Wi-Fi network (named FUJIFILM-…).
  3. Fuji Import connects automatically. Press OK on the camera if it asks.

Settings

Windows Photos and OneDrive pick up photos in your Pictures folder. For Lightroom Classic or Photoshop Elements, choose a folder they watch. Tokens: {yyyy} {MM} {dd} {HH} {mm} {ss} {camera} {original}.
