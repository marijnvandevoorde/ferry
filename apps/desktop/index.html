Connect your camera

Join the camera's Wi-Fi network and Ferry connects by itself.

Waiting for your camera to send photos (PC AutoSave)

  1. On the camera, turn on Wi-Fi the way you would for its phone app.
  2. On this computer, join the camera's Wi-Fi network. Its name usually includes the brand or model.
  3. Ferry connects automatically. Press OK on the camera if it asks.
Where is Wi-Fi on my camera?
  • Canon: the Wi-Fi menu, as for the Camera Connect app.
  • Fujifilm: Playback, then Wireless Communication (on some models the Wi-Fi button).
  • GoPro: Preferences → Connections → Connect Device → GoPro Quik. The network name and password are under Camera Info.
  • Olympus / OM System: as for the OI.Share or OM Image Share app.
  • Panasonic Lumix: as for the Image App or LUMIX Sync app.
  • Ricoh GR: as for the GR WORLD or Image Sync app.
  • Sony: Send to Smartphone in the playback menu.

    Presets:

    Ferry lets go of the camera after the import. Turn the camera off and this computer goes back to your usual Wi-Fi.

    PC AutoSave

    Ferry then starts at login and keeps running in the tray, so the camera can send photos any time.

    Fujifilm cameras: the camera joins your Wi-Fi and sends new photos to this computer, so the computer stays online. Once, on the camera: Set Up → Connection Setting → PC AutoSave Setting. Join your network (Simple Setup uses your router's WPS button, no password to type), then register this computer. After that, choose PC AutoSave in the camera's playback menu to send photos.

    Also upload to Immich (optional)

    Windows Photos, OneDrive, Shotwell and GNOME Photos pick up photos in your Pictures folder. Lightroom Classic (Auto Import), Photoshop Elements (watched folders), digiKam and darktable can watch a folder: choose it here and set Subfolders to “No subfolders” for Lightroom.
