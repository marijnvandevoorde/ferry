**Description**

> Ferry copies the photos and videos on your camera to your Mac over Wi-Fi. No cable, no card reader.
>
> Turn on the camera's Wi-Fi the way you would for its phone app, join its network, and Ferry finds the camera and shows what's on the card. Click Import New and only the photos you haven't imported yet are copied, checked and put where you want them.
>
> • Only new photos: Ferry remembers what it imported, even after you delete photos from your Mac.
> • Where you want them: Apple Photos (with an album), any folder, a folder Lightroom watches, or your own Immich server.
> • Safe copies: every file is checked against what the camera reported, and Ferry warns you before your disk fills up.
> • Pick on the camera: send photos you select on the camera, or import only the ones you protected.
> • Over your home Wi-Fi: Fujifilm cameras can send new photos with PC AutoSave, so your Mac stays online.
> • Stays out of the way: menu bar mode, notifications, and an option to disconnect when the import is done.
>
> Tested on the Fujifilm X-T20, and in an emulator on more Fujifilm and Canon models. Other Fujifilm, Canon, GoPro, Olympus / OM System, Ricoh, Sony and Panasonic cameras with Wi-Fi are supported experimentally; the list of cameras is on the website.
>
> Free and open source (MIT). Ferry collects no data: it only talks to your camera and to where you import to.

**Notes for App Review**

> Ferry imports photos from a camera's own Wi-Fi network, so a full test needs a Wi-Fi camera (Fujifilm X series, Canon EOS, GoPro and others). Without one, the app shows its connect screen with instructions. A demo video of a full import is at <link>. The app is open source; the camera emulator used for testing is described at https://github.com/marijnvandevoorde/ferry#building.
